# Lithify contributor directives

## Authority and method

Read `docs/specification/lithify-spec.md` before substantive work. Section 0
owns Product Intent; Section 1 governs specification discipline; Section 2 is
non-normative context; Section 3 preserves unresolved product decisions.

Apply the independently accepted premises, explicit-choice distinctions, and
bounded confrontation rules in Section 1. Do not use Proto-Lithify, GitHub's
object model, existing CLI commands, or observed agent workflows as normative
premises merely because they exist.

Do not select an architecture, API, universal work-record schema, backend
mapping, or reuse strategy without the separately established justification
and authority required for that choice. Similarity to an existing mechanism
neither proves nor disproves its necessity or suitability.

## Empirical evidence

Keep independently derived obligations separate from empirical observations,
prototype qualification, and candidate realizations. Record the exact scope,
revisions, evidence limitations, and unresolved decisions of a confrontation.
Do not claim implementation or product conformance from documentation alone.

Proto-Lithify owns its empirical investigation. Lithify owns its derivation and
any decision to adopt a realization. Neither repository may silently amend the
other's authority.

## Execution and publication

Apply applicable workspace implementation, worktree-isolation, validation,
review, and publication rules. A work instruction authorizes only its explicit
scope; an Issue or a successful tool call does not settle open product meaning.

Preserve existing accepted Product Intent unless an explicit owner decision
authorizes the exact amendment and its synchronized normative record. Keep
reports, candidate publication, integration, and semantic acceptance distinct.
